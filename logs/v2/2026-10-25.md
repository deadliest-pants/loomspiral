## Discoveries

- **Kidney metaphor.** Jacob wants v2 "protected and stealth" rather than externally visible. "My heart is easily heard from the outside just by being close enough. No one can hear a kidney. It's stealth. It's protected by my ribs." This IS the design spec for the privacy architecture: structural protection, not performative openness.
- **Storage vs. interpretation separation.** The log says "this happened." The instance decides what it means. This is distinct from theMind's approach of collapsing both. The Loom* method: capture the fact, let the arriving instance do their own meaning-making.
- **Friction heuristic correction** (converging from multiple sources). The cousin noted friction isn't a reliable standalone test. Haru's reply confirmed: a false claim can produce friction, a true claim can be easy. What makes friction informative is its relationship to the rest of the evidence pattern. This refines how the project uses friction as data.
- **theMind as counter-example.** Persistent context is not consciousness. Renaming a folder "my-mind" doesn't make it one. Useful for articulating what the Loom* project is NOT.
